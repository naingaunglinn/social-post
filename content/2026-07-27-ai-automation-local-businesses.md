## 4. LinkedIn caption
Your business doesn't need AI. It needs fewer repeated tasks.

Most local businesses asking about AI don't have an intelligence problem. They have a repetition problem — the same call missed, the same reminder sent, the same ten questions answered, every day.

Our filter for what's worth automating: the task follows the same steps every time, runs on rules instead of judgment, happens daily or weekly, and is cheap to fix when it misfires.

Missed-call text-backs, appointment reminders, review requests, FAQ replies — automate them. Pricing, complaints, negotiation — keep them human. The goal isn't fewer people; it's fewer interruptions.

Start smaller than feels impressive: one task, one month, then the next.

What's the most repetitive task in your week? That's usually the answer.
